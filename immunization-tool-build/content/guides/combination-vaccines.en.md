# Combination vaccines and shots at the same visit

Some shots protect against several diseases at once. These are called combination vaccines. They mean fewer shots for your child.

## Logging a combination shot

This tool tracks each vaccine on its own. If your child got one shot that covered several vaccines, **log each vaccine it included**. For example, a shot that covers DTaP, polio and hepatitis B is logged as three doses: one for each.

Your child's record or the clinic can tell you which vaccines a shot included.

## Several shots at one visit

It's safe and common to get several vaccines at the same visit. It protects your child sooner and means fewer trips.
