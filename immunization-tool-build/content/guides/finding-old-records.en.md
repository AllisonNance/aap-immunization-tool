# Finding old records

If you're not sure which vaccines your child has had, these places may have records:

- Your pediatrician's office, or past doctors' offices
- Your state immunization registry (your pediatrician or local health department can help you reach it)
- Your local health department
- Past schools or childcare programs
- Baby books or family records

## Adding what you find

- [Scan a record](app:scan) to add several doses at once.
- Or add doses one at a time from [Add to record](app:add).
- If a record is from another country, see [Vaccines given outside the US](/vaccines/guides/vaccines-outside-us).

A list of state immunization registries will be added here by the AAP.
