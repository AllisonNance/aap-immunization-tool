# When a dose is postponed

Sometimes a dose is put off, for example because your child has a moderate or severe illness. A mild cold is usually not a reason to wait.

## What happens next

- Set a new target date for the dose in your child's record.
- Doses that come after it may move back, to keep the right amount of time between them.
- The series doesn't need to start over.

Ask your pediatrician when it's a good time to reschedule.
