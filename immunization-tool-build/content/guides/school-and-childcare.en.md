# Vaccines for school and childcare

Kids in school and childcare spend their days close together, so illnesses spread easily. Vaccines protect your child and the children around them, including babies too young for some vaccines.

## All recommended vaccines matter

Schools and childcare programs set their own rules, and some vaccines may not be on their list. That doesn't make them less important. **Rotavirus, HPV and hepatitis A** protect your child too, whether or not a school asks for them.

## Getting ready

- Check your child's record for doses that are due now.
- Ask your pediatrician for a copy of your child's official record.
- Learn more on [HealthyChildren.org](https://www.healthychildren.org).
