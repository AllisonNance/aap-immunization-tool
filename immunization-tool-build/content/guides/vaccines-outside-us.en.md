# Vaccines given outside the US

Vaccines given in other countries count, but names, dates and schedules can look different. Some vaccines, like BCG, aren't part of the US schedule.

## Adding them

- If you recognize a vaccine, add it to your child's record as usual.
- If you can't find it, [add a custom vaccine](app:custom). Copy the name exactly as written, and the country.
- Dates may be written day first (15/02/2024). Pick the month by name so there's no mix-up.

## What to ask your pediatrician

- Which of these vaccines count toward the US schedule?
- Does my child need any extra or repeat doses?
- Is there anything my child still needs because of where they lived?
